Your words stay with you

Aurelia by Peak Habit Studio stores your words, favorites, preferences and activity only on this device. No account, ads, analytics or cloud synchronization. Android uses installed offline speech voices; availability and quality depend on your device. Export creates a file only where you choose to save it. Erase local data or uninstall to delete app data. Sessions pause when you leave the app and resume only when you choose. Aurelia supports everyday reflection and relaxation; it is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. For concerns about your health, consult a qualified professional.
