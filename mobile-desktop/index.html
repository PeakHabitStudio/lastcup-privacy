LastCup — Privacy Policy

Publisher: Peak Habit Studio. Updated: 5 October 2026. This notice covers LastCup for Android, Wear OS, Windows, Xbox, iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch.

Information kept on your device

LastCup stores the drinks you enter, estimated caffeine amounts, sleep ratings, bedtime, sensitivity and optional health settings locally on your device. Optional pregnancy, contraceptive use, smoking, age and caffeine-metabolism settings only adjust the local estimate. The app has no user account, advertising SDK or analytics and does not upload these entries to Peak Habit Studio or another server.

Device features

Reminders are scheduled locally by the operating system. Exports are written to the location you choose. Sharing an export is your decision. LastCup does not operate its own cloud synchronization or backup service. Device and operating-system backup features are governed separately by your platform settings and the platform provider.

Retention and controls

Your entries and settings remain locally until you erase them in Settings → Your data or clear the app data. Removing the app and its data follows the behavior of your operating system. This release is free and contains no in-app purchases.

Wellness estimates

Caffeine forecasts use estimates and typical servings. They are not laboratory measurements or a guarantee of sleep. LastCup is for general wellness, is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

This page and support

This notice is hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening it sends ordinary web requests to GitHub under its privacy statement. This page has no analytics, advertisements or external scripts.

If you email support, Peak Habit Studio receives your email address and the information you choose to include and uses them to respond. You may request deletion of that correspondence, subject to required record retention.

Support and privacy questions: support@peakhabitstudio.com. Platform correspondence: info@peakhabitstudio.com.
