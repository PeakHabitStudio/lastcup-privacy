CalmPulse Privacy Policy

Peak Habit Studio · 5 October 2026

CalmPulse works offline. Settings, completed sessions, breathing minutes and streak dates stay on your watch. We receive no app data. There are no ads, analytics, accounts, billing, location or heart-rate access.

To delete local data, clear application storage or uninstall. Garmin, Google and the watch platform may process store and crash information under their own policies.
