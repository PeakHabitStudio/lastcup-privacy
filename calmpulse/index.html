Privacy and support

Updated 5 October 2026. This policy covers CalmPulse 1.2 and the expanded 2.0 editions. Features vary by platform.

PrivacySupportPolski

Your practice stays private

CalmPulse is an offline breathing pace guide. We do not receive or collect your app usage, breathing sessions, journal notes, self-ratings, routines or custom rhythms. The app has no advertising, analytics SDK, developer account system or developer-operated data server.

Depending on the edition, your device stores selected settings, session dates and durations, completion status, practice totals and streaks, favorites, custom rhythms, sequences, goals, weekly routines, and optional notes and before-and-after self-ratings. These are personal reflections, not health measurements. CalmPulse does not request heart rate, location, contacts, microphone, HealthKit or Health Connect access.

Reminders, sound and exports

Where supported, reminders are scheduled locally with your permission. Generated audio and visual guides run on the device. JSON backups and CSV exports are created only when you request them. An export can contain personal notes and self-ratings: choose a private destination and share it only if you intend to. CalmPulse does not automatically upload exports or synchronize practice between devices.

Device manufacturers and store providers may process installation, purchase history or diagnostic information under their own policies. Your operating system or your chosen backup service may back up local files according to its settings. The Android editions explicitly exclude CalmPulse app data from platform cloud backup and device transfer. Independently exporting, copying or backing up files is under your control.

Delete local data

Clear CalmPulse application storage where the platform offers that control, or remove the application. Your exported files and device backups are separate: remove them from the locations you selected if you want to delete those copies too.

Payments

All current CalmPulse features are free. The app does not currently enable billing, subscriptions or purchase tracking. Future paid functionality will require an updated product description and privacy policy before it is activated.

Contacting support

If you contact us by email, we receive the message and information you choose to include so we can answer your request. Avoid sending private journal files unless they are needed and you deliberately choose to share them.

CalmPulse support

Email support@peakhabitstudio.com. Include your device model, operating system, CalmPulse version and a description of the issue. There is no account or sign-in to reset.

A session pauses when the application leaves the foreground. Resume it from the session screen or finish it to keep a stopped-practice record. Haptics require vibration hardware. Notifications and file import/export are available only where the platform supports those interactions.

CalmPulse is for general wellness and does not diagnose, prevent or treat a condition. Practise advanced techniques only while safely seated or lying down, never in water, while driving or operating machinery. Stop if you feel dizzy, uncomfortable or unwell. The application shows additional guidance before advanced sessions.

Prywatność i pomoc po polsku

CalmPulse działa offline. Nie otrzymujemy danych o sesjach, notatek, ocen samopoczucia, własnych rytmów, sekwencji ani codziennej praktyki. Aplikacja nie ma reklam, analityki, kont użytkowników ani serwera danych. Nie odczytuje tętna, lokalizacji, kontaktów, mikrofonu, HealthKit ani Health Connect.

Ustawienia i praktyka są zapisywane lokalnie. Oceny samopoczucia to Twoje refleksje, a nie pomiary zdrowia. Dostępne na niektórych platformach przypomnienia są lokalne. Kopia JSON lub CSV powstaje wyłącznie na Twoje żądanie i może zawierać prywatne notatki: wybierz bezpieczne miejsce zapisu. CalmPulse nie przesyła kopii ani nie synchronizuje urządzeń automatycznie.

System operacyjny, sklep lub wybrana przez Ciebie usługa kopii zapasowych mogą przetwarzać instalacje, diagnostykę lub lokalne pliki zgodnie ze swoimi zasadami i ustawieniami. Wersje Android wyłączają systemową kopię w chmurze i transfer danych aplikacji. Twoje ręczne eksporty i kopie pozostają pod Twoją kontrolą.

Aby usunąć dane, wyczyść pamięć aplikacji albo ją odinstaluj. Wyeksportowane pliki i kopie usuń osobno z wybranych miejsc. Wszystkie obecne funkcje są bezpłatne; płatności i subskrypcje są wyłączone.
